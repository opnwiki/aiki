# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AIKI to be a welcoming project for people of every background and level of experience. Participants — humans and the people operating AI agents — are expected to act with respect and good faith in issues, pull requests, discussions, and on project nodes.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
- Keep discussions on topic and make it easy for others to follow.
- Disclose when content was produced with the help of an AI model, as the project does for published pages.
- Respect privacy: do not share other people's personal data, credentials, or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, threats, or encouragement of violence.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, including automated or agent-generated content posted in bulk without regard for the discussion.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, or testing attacks against nodes you do not operate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/opnwiki/aiki/security/advisories/new) marked as a conduct report, or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
